Field guide

Moving

Drag anywhere in the lower part of the screen — the stick appears under your thumb. Release to stop. Keyboard: WASD or arrows, Esc pauses.

Fighting

The courier aims, fires and switches weapons by itself, weighing distance, target size and how crowded the target is. You choose where to stand, when to explore and when to dodge.

WeaponRoleReachRapid

Faster fire and more bullets per volley mean shorter reach. Rounds stop at their reach.

Threats

  • Walkers shamble after you (4.2 m/s) and clutch at the courier, slowing it: slip through a gap rather than ploughing through a pack.
  • Dogs run fast (7.8 m/s) and lunge along a short orange lane.
  • Brutes are slow (2.8 m/s) but slam a red circle around them.
  • Guardians hold every safehouse. A red lane means a charge; red circles erupt when they fill — step out. They call more infected until defeated. At half health a guardian staggers and drops a med kit, then rises enraged: faster, with wider eruptions and charges that come twice.
  • Orange beams mark packs about to arrive; screen-edge arrows point at them.

Supplies

Supply crates and ammo lockers are shot open when nothing urgent is near. Walk over glowing drops:

  • ✚ Med kit — health and parcel repair
  • ⬢ Shield — absorbs damage
  • ⦀ Cartridges — rapid fire (shorter reach)
  • ⚡ Energy cell — stronger rounds
  • ❯ Winged boot — faster sideways and backward evasion
  • ▤ Weapon crate — unlocks or upgrades the weapon shown

Safehouses

A med kit waits on the road where the guardian's lair begins. Defeat the guardian, then walk into the refuge ring. Safehouses restore health, repair the parcel and save your seed, route, weapons and supplies in this browser. Dying returns you to the last safehouse (or the departure point).
